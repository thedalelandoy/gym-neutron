## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

Beginners don't avoid the gym because of price. They avoid it because they don't know what to do
and are scared of being watched while they figure it out. So the campaign sells **certainty and
safety**, not access. The front-end offer is free and stacked with everything that removes fear
(a coach on day one, a written plan, a beginner-only hour, check-ins). The ad budget is sized to
fill exactly the 100 slots the trainers can handle. The money comes on the back end, when
graduates of the 30-day challenge buy coaching packs at a launch discount.

## 2. Avatar

**"The nervous first-timer."** 18–30, student or young professional living within about 8 km of
the gym (assumed launch area: Calamba, Laguna). Has watched workout videos, maybe bought a
membership once and stopped going. Biggest fears, in their words:

1. "I don't know how to use the machines and I'll look stupid."
2. "Everyone there is already fit and will judge me."
3. "I'll pay and quit again."

## 3. The offer — "The Confidence Catalyst: 30-Day Beginner Challenge"

Naming: a reason (launch), the avatar (beginners), the outcome (confidence), a time frame
(30 days) and a container word (challenge).

**Value equation check.** Raise the dream outcome and the perceived likelihood of getting it;
cut the time to the first win and the effort and sacrifice.

| Lever | How the offer moves it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome ↑ | Walk in on day 30 knowing exactly what to do, with a plan and visible progress |
| Perceived likelihood ↑ | A real coach on day one, a written roadmap, two check-ins, a beginner-only hour |
| Time delay ↓ | First win on day one: the orientation ends with your first full workout done |
| Effort & sacrifice ↓ | ₱0, no contract, quiet off-peak hours, machine how-to videos on every station |

**Value stack** (values are typical Calamba/Laguna retail prices, used as anchors):

| # | Item | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 days of Starter access (off-peak gym floor) | ₱1,500 |
| 2 | 1-on-1, 60-minute orientation with a Standard Tier coach | ₱500 |
| 3 | Your 30-day training roadmap, written for your goal | ₱800 |
| 4 | Rookie Hour: beginner-only small-group sessions, twice a week | ₱1,200 |
| 5 | Machine cheat sheets: scan the QR on any station for a 30-second how-to | ₱300 |
| 6 | Day-14 and Day-30 progress check-ins with your coach | ₱600 |
| | **Total value** | **₱4,900** |
| | **Price during the launch** | **₱0** |

**Guarantee — the No-Judgment Promise.** If anyone at Gym Neutron makes you feel judged, tell the
front desk and your next coaching session is on us. No sales pitch during the orientation.

**Show-up reward (conditional bonus).** Finish 12 workouts in 30 days and you graduate with 20% off
your first coaching pack. This builds the habit and leads straight into the back end.

**Bring-a-buddy.** Register with a friend; both get the full challenge (each uses one slot).
Beginners are braver in pairs, and every buddy is a lead the ads didn't pay for.

**Scarcity (real).** 100 slots, because that is how many one-on-one orientations the Standard Tier
coaches can deliver in November. **Urgency (real).** Registration closes November 30, 2026,
11:59 PM (PHT). No fake countdown resets and no fake "slots left" counter.

**Terms carried over from the original plan:** new members only, first 100 applicants, valid local
ID or proof of residence at check-in, orientation with Standard Tier coaches only (Elite/Premium
if the difference is paid), no orientations 5–8 PM on weekdays, no cash value, not transferable,
not combinable with other promos.

## 4. Funnel (how a stranger becomes a paying client)

1. **Ad** (Meta or TikTok) calls out the avatar and the fear → tap.
2. **Landing page** (this site) → registration form: name, email, mobile, preferred off-peak
   time, consent.
3. **Speed to lead.** The front desk calls or texts within 5 minutes during staffed hours (a lead
   contacted in 5 minutes is far likelier to book than one contacted the next day).
4. **Book the orientation** in an off-peak slot. Reminder texts 24 hours and 2 hours before.
5. **Orientation** → the member leaves with the roadmap and their first workout done.
6. **Rookie Hour + Day-14 check-in** keep them coming back through the drop-off zone (weeks 2–3).
7. **Day-30 graduation check-in** → show progress, then offer coaching packs with the 20%
   show-up reward.

## 5. Budget and campaign numbers (₱30,000 total, November 1–30, 2026)

| Platform | Daily | 30 days | Assumed cost per landing-page visit | Visitors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta (Facebook + Instagram) | ₱600 | ₱18,000 | ₱7.50 | 2,400 |
| TikTok | ₱400 | ₱12,000 | ₱5.00 | 2,400 |
| **Total** | **₱1,000** | **₱30,000** | | **4,800** |

- **Forecast leads** = 4,800 visitors × 5% assumed conversion = **240 leads**
- **Lead conversion** = 240 ÷ 4,800 × 100% = **5%**
- **Cost per lead** = ₱30,000 ÷ 240 = **₱125**

Down the funnel: 240 leads × 60% book = 144 bookings × 70% show = **≈101 show-ups**, which fills the
**100 slots**. Cost per show-up ≈ ₱300.

Back end: 25% of 100 graduates buy an 8-session Standard pack (₱400 × 8 = ₱3,200, minus 20% =
₱2,560) → **25 × ₱2,560 = ₱64,000** in coaching revenue → **2.1× return on ad spend** within
60 days, before counting anyone who stays on longer.

| Scenario | Page conversion | Leads | Cost per lead | Show-ups (60% × 70%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 3% | 144 | ₱208 | 60 |
| Expected | 5% | 240 | ₱125 | 100 (capped) |
| Stretch | 7% | 336 | ₱89 | 100 + waitlist |

**Revised SMART goal.** Fill all 100 Confidence Catalyst slots (100 completed orientations)
between November 1 and 30, 2026, at no more than ₱125 per lead on a ₱30,000 ad budget, and
convert at least 25 graduates to paid coaching packs by December 31, 2026.

**If traffic is high but leads are low** (the pitch question): the ad works and the page doesn't.
Check in this order: (1) message match between the ad hook and the hero headline, (2) mobile load
speed, (3) form friction (fields, errors), (4) offer clarity above the fold. Change one thing at a
time and compare page conversion day over day.

**Weekly KPIs:** cost per landing-page visit, page conversion ≥ 4%, time to first contact
< 5 minutes, booking rate ≥ 60%, show rate ≥ 70%, cost per show-up ≤ ₱350.

## 6. Site plan

### Course requirements this site must meet

- HTML/CSS/JS in separate files, semantic elements, relative paths, alt text, responsive.
- **JS feature 1:** name/email/consent validation with clear feedback; simulated success only,
  nothing stored or sent.
- **JS feature 2:** countdown to a fixed date (Nov 30, 2026, 11:59:59 PM PHT) with an expired
  promotion state. Testable with `?now=2026-12-01T00:00:00+08:00`.
- **JS feature 3 (choose one):** a quiz — "Select your element" — straight from the tagline.
- At least 5 documented test cases; the expired-state test is mandatory.

### Look

- Brand: black, blue and red (the plan's dark variant), with white type.
  Blue `#2f6bff`, red `#ff2b3d`, near-black `#07080c`.
- Type: Archivo (variable width). Extra-expanded heavy weight for display, normal width for body.
- Apple-style glass for UI panels (`backdrop-filter`), real refractive glass in 3D for the orbit rings.
- One signature moment: **the value stack loads a barbell.** Every bonus you scroll past slides a
  plate onto the bar. The rest stays quiet and precise.

### 3D scene (Three.js r170, GSAP 3.12.5 ScrollTrigger, Lenis 1.1.0)

Archetype: **object showcase**, a fixed canvas behind the page, camera and objects driven by one
scrubbed scroll timeline, every value damped.

Equipment, all procedural, modelled with real proportions:

- **Bumper plates** (lathe profile): 450 mm diameter, steel hub with a 50 mm bore, recessed face,
  raised rim. Red 25 kg (thicker), blue 20 kg, white 5 kg (thin).
- **Olympic barbell:** 2.2 m, 28 mm knurled shaft, 50 mm chrome sleeves, collars at the joins.
- **Hex dumbbell:** six-sided rubber heads, chrome knurled handle, chrome collars.
- **Kettlebell** (lathe body with a flat base, swept handle with two horns): blue competition finish.

Storyboard:

| Section | Scene | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | **The atom.** Kettlebell nucleus; plates and dumbbells orbit on three tilted glass rings (blue, red, white). Mouse parallax. | "Select your element. Build your strength." + offer line + CTA |
| Fear | Orbits loosen and drift apart, camera pulls back, light dims. | "The hardest rep is walking in." Three fears |
| Offer (pinned) | **The bar loads.** Barbell centre frame; each stack item slides a plate pair on. Total value counts up. | Six stack items → ₱4,900 → ₱0 |
| How it works | Loaded bar lifts and turns; camera orbits. | Four steps: register → orientation → 30 days → graduation |
| Element quiz | Dumbbells settle into a grid of element tiles. | 4 questions → Fe / O / Ca / Nu result |
| Lock your spot | Kettlebell close, slow turn. | Countdown, form, guarantee |
| FAQ + footer | Scene fades to dark. | Terms, contact |

Performance and fallbacks:

- Pixel ratio capped at 2 (1.5 on phones); bloom, grain and glass transmission on desktop only.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no Lenis, no scrub, one still frame per section.
- If WebGL or the CDN fails, or the page is opened from `file://`, the 3D is skipped and a CSS
  backdrop shows instead. The form, countdown and quiz live in a plain script that never depends on
  the 3D.
