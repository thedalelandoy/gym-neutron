## What we did together

1. **Ideation.** Chose the gym-beginner problem, named the brand Gym Neutron and wrote the
   tagline, "Select your element. Build your strength."
2. **Marketing strategy.** Defined the nervous first-timer as the target customer, built the
   Confidence Catalyst offer and its value stack, and set the SMART goal.
3. **Budget and numbers.** Split the ₱30,000 between Meta and TikTok and worked out forecast
   leads, lead conversion and cost per lead.
4. **Copywriting.** Wrote the ad hooks, the landing page copy and the offer terms.
5. **Design.** Picked the black, blue and red look, the type and the glass panels, and storyboarded
   the scroll-driven 3D scene.
6. **Website building.** Wrote the HTML, CSS and JavaScript: form validation, the countdown with an
   expired state, the "Select your element" quiz and the Three.js gym equipment.
7. **Testing.** Ran 16 test cases on desktop and phone sizes, including the expired-offer test.
8. **Pitch.** Built the slides and rehearsed the demo and the Q&A.

## How we used AI

Our professor allowed AI tools as long as we stay creative and understand the code. We used
Claude (Anthropic) as a design and coding partner: to draft and tighten copy, generate and review
code, and check the site at different screen sizes. Every idea, number and line of code was
reviewed by the group, and each of us can explain how the site works and how it was built. The
walkthrough we studied is in `docs/PITCH-SCRIPT.md`, under "How the code works".
