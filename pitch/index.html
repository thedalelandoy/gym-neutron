Testing and contingency

High traffic, low leads?
The page is the problem.

  1. Does the headline repeat the ad's hook word for word?
  2. Does it load fast on a phone over mobile data?
  3. Is the form short, with clear error messages?
  4. Are the offer and deadline visible without scrolling?

16 of 16 test cases passed, including the expired-offer test, on six screen sizes.

Weekly KPIs: page conversion ≥ 4% · first contact < 5 min · booking ≥ 60% · show rate ≥ 70% · cost per show-up ≤ ₱350
