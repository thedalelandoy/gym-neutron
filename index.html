    Questions, answered.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There's no card and no contract. The 30 days, orientation, roadmap, Rookie Hour and check-ins all cost ₱0. Afterwards you can stay on our free limited-access tier or buy a coaching pack. It's your choice.

    I'm out of shape. Is this really for me?

    It was built for you. Every session starts where you are, and Rookie Hour is beginners only. Nobody expects you to know anything on day one.

    What happens after 30 days?

    At your Day-30 check-in your coach shows you your progress. If you finished 12 workouts, you get 20% off your first coaching pack. There's no pressure to buy.

    When can I train?

    Starter access covers off-peak hours: weekdays before 5 PM and after 8 PM, and all day on weekends.

    What should I bring?

    Comfortable clothes, rubber shoes, a water bottle, a small towel and a valid ID.

    Offer terms

    • New members only, limited to the first 100 people who register.
    • Bring a valid local ID or proof of residence at check-in.
    • The free orientation is with a Standard Tier coach. Elite or Premium coaches are available if you pay the difference.
    • Orientations can't be booked 5 to 8 PM, Monday to Friday.
    • No cash value. Not transferable, and can't be combined with other discounts or corporate perks.
